> The system maintains parallel interpretation paths derived from shared observations.  
> These paths are updated iteratively and may diverge or converge over time, rather than being reduced to a single resolved state prematurely.  
> 
> Authoritative outputs and directives are produced as a time-bound coalescence of these paths, weighted according to their state at that point, and shall be acted upon within that context.

# Ethics & Use Boundaries

This system ingests and represents real-world conditions across multiple sources.  
Those representations may be incomplete, inconsistent, delayed, or in tension with one another.

> [!WARNING]
>
> **Misuse or misinterpretation of outputs **WILL** influence life-safety outcomes.**
> 
> This system **DOES NOT** resolve uncertainty or adjudicate conflicting inputs.  
> Treating outputs as complete, authoritative, or decision-ready can produce incorrect conclusions and downstream harm.
> 
> *If you cannot account for these properties, you* **MAY NOT** *use this system.*

---

## Intended Use

- Preserve observational fidelity across heterogeneous sources  
- Maintain temporal and spatial resolution  
- Expose, rather than collapse, disagreement and uncertainty  
- Support informed human decision-making  

---

## Limitations

This system does not guarantee:

- accuracy  
- completeness  
- timeliness  
- consistency between sources  
- resolution of conflicting inputs  

***Uncertainty is preserved as a feature.***

Outputs are representations of observed data. They are not self-validating.

---

## Safety-Critical Contexts

> [!CAUTION]
> In safety-relevant use:
> - You **MUST** independently verify critical inputs  
> - You **MUST** maintain fallback procedures  
> - This system **MUST NOT** be treated as a sole verified source of truth  

---

## Prohibited Use

This project and its derivatives **SHALL NOT** be used to:

- conduct surveillance  
- support coercive or carceral systems  
- target vulnerable populations  
- represent outputs as inherently authoritative  

---

## Final Condition

If your use requires guaranteed correctness or inherent authority, this system is not appropriate for that use case.
