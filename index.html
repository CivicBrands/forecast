Location details & privacy
Default Kansas City area, 30 mile radius.

Your location is not stored. “Use my location” asks your browser for coordinates and sends them with a single request, only to pick which weather stations, air-quality monitors and parks to read. They are used to build that one response and are then discarded — not written to the database, not logged, not associated with any identifier, and never shared. Nothing is recorded that could reconstruct where you were. Declining the prompt simply keeps the default Kansas City view, and you can clear it any time with the KC button.
