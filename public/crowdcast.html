The live Park Crowd-Cast is now served at forecast.civicbrands.org/crowdcast.

Why this file is a stub. It previously held an early static prototype built on seed estimates. That prototype carried a claim we have since retracted: that low-crowd parks sit “east of Troost” and lack canopy because it “was never planted.” Measurement contradicted both halves — HOLC grade is not equivalent to a position relative to Troost, and park-interior canopy varies little by grade.

The corrected finding, and the neighborhood-level measurements behind it, are documented in docs/compendium/parks.md. This stub exists so the retracted version cannot be served or shared from a stale file.
